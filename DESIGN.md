# Film design

The intended audience is young Ethiopian developers and students outside the big cities, along with family and followers. The film is designed to be watched on a phone. The storyboard uses humor to make the difficult parts of learning to code relatable, then closes with the phone call to the filmmaker's mother. Each shot has a reason in [storyboard.json](storyboard.json). The loading ring and the promise to call are recurring visual and narrative cues.
