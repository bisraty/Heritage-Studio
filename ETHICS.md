# Ethics and consent

## Recorded consent

On 24 November 2026 (East Africa Time), the filmmaker confirmed consent in the project conversation to be depicted as an AI-generated illustration in *Hirna to Addis* and to have a synthetic narrator tell his story in the first person. He also agreed to record that consent here. The supplied portrait informed the written character description. The image itself was kept local.

The mother's consent is pending. Shot 4 shows the bag handoff without faces, and she stays offscreen during the phone call in shot 9.

The filmmaker also authorized sending the written character description to Cloudflare FLUX to generate four reference options. The portrait itself was excluded from those requests.
